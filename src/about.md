From a very early age, Kaz has been fascinated by mermaids and the ocean.
Growing up in Tasmania fifteen minutes from the beach, she fell in love with
our pristine beaches and coastlines — but a lifelong phobia of sharks kept her
out of water past her waist. It wasn't until she decided to face her fears and
take on a new challenge that she started researching freediving, and then
discovered mermaiding.

{% image "src/assets/images/uploads/kaz-rockpool.jpg", "Kaz reclining on coastal rocks beside a rockpool, wearing a pink and aqua mermaid tail", "photo", "(min-width: 1024px) 900px, 100vw" %}

Kaz has travelled extensively to the mainland and trained with some of
Australia's top mermaid instructors and mermaid instructor trainers. She is
thrilled to have made her own dreams come true by becoming not only Tasmania's
first PADI Mermaid and professional mermaid performer, but also the first
Mermaid Instructor on our beautiful island. She loves to share her passion for
mermaid diving and the beauty of fluid, peaceful underwater movement — be it
in the pool or the ocean.

Kaz also performs as a real live mermaid at community events, corporate events
and birthday parties. As a primary school teacher with 17 years of experience
and a degree in Performing Arts, she is highly skilled at working with
children — and at creating the illusion for them that mermaids are real!
